Behind the Lens

I'm Alvin Ouyang — a visual storyteller based in New York, specializing in Sports and Portraits. I believe the best moments aren't the ones you plan, but the ones that happen when people forget the camera is there. That's why I focus on capturing real reactions, raw emotions, and the energy that makes every story unique.

I'm currently working for Stony Brook Athletics, creating highlight reels, hype videos, and game recaps featured on official channels. From the roar of a championship crowd to noticing the small details in sports, I bring the same dedication to every project. My approach is simple: show up prepared, capture the moment, and deliver work that makes people feel something. Whether it's a game-winning goal, a senior portrait, or a brand promo — I'm there to tell your story. Let's create something real.

Let's create together.
